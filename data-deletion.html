Data Deletion and Account Disconnection

Effective date: October 30, 2026

You may request disconnection of a connected social account and deletion of personal information associated with your MALAH NET use.

How to submit a request

  1. Email ELMALAHE67@GMAIL.COM from an address you control.
  2. Use the subject line “MALAH NET — Data Deletion Request”.
  3. Identify the connected platform (for example, TikTok) and provide the account username or another non-secret identifier sufficient to locate the connection.
  4. Do not send passwords, access tokens, refresh tokens, verification codes, or other authentication secrets.

What happens next

We will verify the request as reasonably necessary, disconnect the relevant integration where applicable, and delete or anonymize eligible information under our control, subject to legal, security, and record-keeping requirements. We will respond to the request using the contact details supplied. Information held independently by TikTok or another third-party platform must be managed through that platform’s own privacy and account controls.

Revoke TikTok access

You can also review and revoke third-party app permissions from the relevant platform’s account settings. Revoking permission at the platform may stop future API access but does not by itself guarantee deletion of information already held by MALAH NET; submit a separate deletion request using the contact method above.
